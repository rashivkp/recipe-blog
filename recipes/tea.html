Tea

A refreshing milk tea in 2 min

Recipe 1

Ingredients

  • 3/4 glass water
  • 1/2 glass milk
  • 3 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 teaspoon tea-powder
  • 1 cardamon

Instructions

  1. Boil the water.
  2. After the water is boiled , add sugar and tea-powder
  3. Mix with the milk, and stir.
  4. Take 2 glasses and transfer the liquid from one glass to another a few times.
  5. Pour it into a cup/glass, and garnish with cardamon

Recipe 2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon tea powder
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons sugar

Instructions

  1. Boil the water in a saucepan.
  2. Add the tea powder and simmer for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the milk and bring it to a boil.
  4. Add sugar and mix well.
  5. Strain the tea into a cup.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy.
