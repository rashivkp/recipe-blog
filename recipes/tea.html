☕ Homemade Milk Tea Recipe

A simple and delicious cup of hot milk tea that you can make at home.

Preparation Time: 5 minutes
Cooking Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 2 cups

Ingredients

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons tea powder
  • 2–3 teaspoons sugar
  • 2 cardamom pods (optional)
  • Small piece of ginger (optional)

Instructions

  1. Pour 2 cups of water into a saucepan.
  2. Add tea powder and bring the water to a boil.
  3. Add crushed cardamom and ginger if you want extra flavor.
  4. Add 1 cup of milk and continue boiling for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Add sugar according to your taste.
  6. Simmer the tea for another 2 minutes.
  7. Strain the tea into cups using a tea strainer.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy your homemade tea! ☕
💡 Tip: For stronger tea, add a little more tea powder and simmer for an extra minute.
