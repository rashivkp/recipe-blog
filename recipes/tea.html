☕ Classic Milk Tea

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon black tea
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1–2 teaspoons sugar
  • 2 cardamom pods (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil one cup of water in a saucepan.
  2. Add black tea and let it boil for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add milk and sugar.
  4. Add cardamom if you prefer extra flavor.
  5. Simmer for another 2 minutes.
  6. Strain the tea into a cup and serve hot.
