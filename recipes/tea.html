Masala Tea Recipe

Make a flavorful cup of homemade masala tea with simple ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon black tea
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 cardamom pods
  • Small piece of ginger
  • Small piece of cinnamon

Preparation

  1. Boil water with ginger and spices.
  2. Add black tea and simmer for two minutes.
  3. Add milk and sugar.
  4. Boil for another two minutes.
  5. Strain into a cup and serve hot.
