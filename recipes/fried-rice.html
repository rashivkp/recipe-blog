Vegetable Fried Rice

Delicious, quick, and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 small carrot, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup cabbage, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup beans, finely chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2–3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1–2 green chillies, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 spring onions, chopped

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice and let it cool completely. Cold rice works best for fried rice.
  2. Heat oil in a wok or large pan over high heat.
  3. Add the garlic, green chillies, and onion. Stir-fry for 30–60 seconds.
  4. Add the carrot, cabbage, and beans. Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Add the soy sauce, vinegar, and black pepper powder. Mix well.
  6. Add the cooked rice and toss everything together gently.
  7. Add salt to taste and stir-fry for another 1–2 minutes.
  8. Garnish with chopped spring onions.
  9. Serve hot and enjoy your vegetable fried rice.
