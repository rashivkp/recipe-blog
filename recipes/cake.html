Cake-recipe

Soft, chewy, and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Grease and line a cake pan with parchment paper.
  3. Cream the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time and mix well.
  5. Add the vanilla extract and mix until combined.
  6. Sift together the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt.
  7. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, alternating with the milk.
  8. Mix gently until you get a smooth cake batter.
  9. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  10. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan and spread it evenly.
  11. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  12. Allow the cake to cool before removing it from the pan and serving.
